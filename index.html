Behind the Name

Some stories needed
another voice.

Ransom Black wasn’t created to pretend to be somebody else. He’s the part of me that doesn’t always make it into everyday conversation.

The anger. The loyalty. The mistakes. The things I’ve carried longer than I should have. The love for the people I’d walk through hell for. The smartass who probably should’ve kept his mouth shut. And the part of me that still believes a man can be better tomorrow than he was yesterday.

I had stories I wanted to tell, but I was never going to be the guy standing behind a microphone singing them. So I gave them a voice.

His name is Ransom Black.
The songs may come through him. But the stories are mine.

Eric Porter · Creator of Ransom Black
The Artist

Ransom Black

Ransom Black isn’t built around pretending the hard parts never happened. His songs live in the space between the man you were, the man the world expects you to be, and the man you’re still fighting to become.

Rooted in dark country, Americana and Southern rock, Ransom writes about carrying weight, taking the hit, loving hard, getting lost, finding faith and standing back up. The rough edges stay. So do the scars. That’s the point.

“Ransom is inside everybody. We all have a little piece of that outlaw, badass SOB deep down within, just begging to get out.”

Behind the defiance is something quieter: loyalty. The belief that strength doesn’t mean carrying everything alone — and that the right person beside you can turn noise into peace. For Ransom, that person is Marlowe.

Music for the ones who still feel.
