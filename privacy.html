Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Ransom Black website (ransomblackmusic.com), operated by Eric Porter under the informal name MegEricAn Records, collects and uses information. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@ransomblackmusic.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect your email address and name when you subscribe, information you voluntarily submit through The Wall or contact features, and, if you separately choose to join our SMS program once available, your mobile number and records of SMS consent. Basic website usage information may also be collected through analytics and technical logs.

How we use information

We use information to deliver requested email updates, respond to messages, operate and protect the site, understand general website traffic, and, only after separate explicit consent, send Ransom Black promotional SMS updates about music, videos, merchandise and related news.

Services and sharing

We use service providers to operate our website, mailing list, analytics and communications, including Sender for email and planned SMS communications. Providers may process information only as needed to perform these services. We do not sell subscriber information. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect the service.

Email and SMS choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in the email. When the SMS program is active, you can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for assistance. You can also email contact@ransomblackmusic.com. Email subscription does not constitute SMS consent.

Retention and security

We retain information as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, to honor opt-outs, and to meet legal requirements. We use reasonable security measures, but no online system is completely secure.

External sites and updates

Links to music platforms, merchandise stores and social networks lead to services with their own privacy practices. We may update this policy as our services change; the effective date above will be updated when appropriate.
