Artist Bio

About Ransom Black

Ransom Black is a virtual dark-country artist built around a simple idea: there's a Ransom inside everybody.

His music lives somewhere between the barroom and the backroad—where country storytelling meets Americana, blues and the weight of Southern rock. The songs aren't about pretending the scars never happened. They're about what a man becomes because of them.

Ransom writes from a world of mistakes, loyalty, love, faith, regret and redemption. Sometimes it's dark. Sometimes it's loud. Sometimes it's just a man, an acoustic guitar and something he probably should've said years ago.

At the center of it all is strength—not the kind that never breaks, but the kind that gets back up, carries what it can, and keeps moving.

“Ransom is inside everybody. We all have a little piece of that outlaw, badass SOB deep down within, just begging to get out.”
Music for the ones who still feel.
